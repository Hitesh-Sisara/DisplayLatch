A free, open-source BetterDisplay alternative

BetterDisplay is an excellent, very complete display tool. Some features, like disconnecting a display, are in its paid Pro version. If you only need the everyday controls, DisplayLatch does them for free and in the open.

When to choose which

Choose DisplayLatch if you want a small, free, auditable app that keeps your monitors connected and handles brightness, resolution and disconnecting. Choose BetterDisplay if you need HiDPI on non-Retina monitors, virtual screens, picture-in-picture or XDR. Other free tools worth knowing: MonitorControl (brightness and volume keys) and displayplacer (arrangement from the CLI).

Switching from BetterDisplay

If BetterDisplay disconnected a monitor and you can't turn it back on without Pro, DisplayLatch can bring it back. Install it and the monitor reconnects automatically, or run displaylatch fix. Quit BetterDisplay first, so the two apps don't both change the same displays.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free alternative to BetterDisplay?

Yes. DisplayLatch is a free, open-source macOS app (MIT). It handles keeping monitors connected, disconnecting displays, DDC brightness, resolution and a CLI. MonitorControl is another free option for brightness.

Can DisplayLatch reconnect a monitor that BetterDisplay disconnected?

Yes. If BetterDisplay disconnected a monitor and your Pro trial ended, DisplayLatch detects that the monitor is attached but off the desktop and reconnects it.
